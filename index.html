01 — The name

Health through movement.

HK is not an abbreviation for the obvious. The H stands for Health, the K for Kinetic — movement. Together they describe what fitness should be about: improving your health through better movement, and building strength that supports everyday life.

H

Health

What we protect: how you feel, how you recover, and how your body holds up over the next decade — not just next month.

K

Kinetic

How we get there: deliberate movement. Technique, mobility and strength trained in the order your body can handle.

03 — Method

A plan that fits the person.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Your goals, training history, schedule and what your body has been telling you. We start with the person, not the programme.

  2. 02

    Assess

    How you move, what's strong, what's stiff and what needs care. This is where the plan stops being generic.

  3. 03

    Build

    A strong foundation first: technique, mobility, control. Load comes once the pattern is there.

  4. 04

    Progress

    We review, adjust and raise the bar at the pace you're ready for — then take it into everyday life.

06 — The coach

Ten years of listening first.

Over more than ten years in the fitness industry, I've seen how much a person's life can change when they become stronger and more confident in their body.

I started HK Fitness to create a welcoming space where people can learn to move well, train safely and progress at their own pace — whether you're starting for the first time or getting back into exercise.

That experience has taught me to listen first. A good training plan should fit the person — their lifestyle, challenges and goals — and develop as they progress.

Why a bear?

Strength, calmness and steady progress. Powerful, but friendly and approachable — the qualities I want HK Fitness to reflect. It's also a reminder that we often have more strength than we realise. My role is to help you discover it.

07 — Questions

Before you start.

Still unsure? Ask directly — the first conversation is free.

Get in touch
What does HK Fitness stand for?

H stands for Health, and K stands for Kinetic — meaning movement. Together, they reflect what I believe fitness should be about: improving your health through better movement and building strength that supports everyday life.

What does “move pain-free” mean?

It means working towards movement that feels comfortable and confident — whether you're exercising, climbing stairs or playing with your children. My approach focuses on improving movement, mobility and strength through exercises suited to your ability.

When pain or an injury needs medical attention, I encourage you to work with a qualified healthcare professional so your training supports their guidance.

What experience do you bring?

I have over ten years of experience and have coached more than 100 clients, each with different goals, abilities and starting points. That experience has taught me to listen first: a good training plan should fit the person — their lifestyle, challenges and goals — and develop as they progress.

Who is HK Fitness for?

HK Fitness is for people who want personal guidance, a clear plan and a comfortable place to train. I work with beginners, busy adults and older adults who want to build strength, improve mobility and stay active. Strength training also helps support muscle and bone health as we age, so we can continue doing the things we enjoy.

What can I expect when training with you?

Personal attention, clear explanations and a plan built around your starting point. We'll work on technique, build a strong foundation and progress together. Beyond what you lift or what the scale says, I want you to notice the difference in everyday life: moving more comfortably, feeling stronger and trusting your body more.

Where is the studio?

The studio is in Kepong, Kuala Lumpur — 87-1, Jalan 1/17, Taman Fadason, 52000 Kepong. Sessions are by appointment, so message me on WhatsApp first and we'll find a time that works.

Why is there a bear in your logo?

To me, the bear represents strength, calmness and steady progress. It's powerful, but it also feels friendly and approachable — qualities I want HK Fitness to reflect. It's also a reminder that we often have more strength than we realise.

Where can I hear from your clients?

Every client has a different starting point and a different reason for training. Their stories show what progress means to them — from feeling stronger to enjoying activities they once found difficult. Have a look at the client results, or ask and I'll share more.

Do I need experience before I start?

No. Most people start with little or no experience, and that's exactly what the first sessions are designed for. We begin with movement and technique at a pace you're comfortable with, then build from there.
